# Unified typography and compact layouts

The UI now uses one Inter family across Ukrainian, English and German. Instrument Serif and the decorative monospace UI stacks were removed. Inter's Cyrillic and Latin subsets remain enabled. Open Graph artwork and the existing information-only PDFs retain their embedded document fonts; their content was not regenerated by this UI change.

`app/design-system.css` owns the UI family, page/section/card heading scale, gutters, section spacing, panel padding and card radius. Page titles now top out at 56 px; feature headings at 40 px and section headings at 34 px. Existing semantic headings and literal heading utility classes use that scale. Reading text, content, headings, source dates, URLs and download routes remain intact.

The team page has a compact two-column introduction, shorter portrait cards (320 px minimum instead of 480), smaller founder names and role badges, and a smaller John panel. Card radii and spacing align with the shared system. The supplied screenshot previously showed the much taller cards and mixed serif/sans styling.

The pass also covers the homepage, blog, article pages, canton/country guides, services, jobs, planning, travel, community, legal pages, shared navigation, footer and PDF banners. Large minimum heights and padding were reduced where they created empty space. The travel PDF banner minimum changed from 660 to 440 px; guide and article covers are more compact. Homepage and places cinematic scroll sections retain their interactions with shorter 260svh / 240svh timelines instead of 570svh / 500svh. Reduced-motion behavior remains separate.

Responsive layouts retain their existing column changes. Service catalogue and homepage places headers were checked specifically to keep single-column mobile rules. Theme colors and keyboard focus treatment remain theme-aware.
