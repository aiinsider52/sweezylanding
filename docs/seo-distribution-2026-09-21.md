## 2. Facebook: city or canton

Moving to Canton Zurich does not necessarily mean registering at the City of Zurich's residents office. Start with the municipality where you will actually live.

## 3. Telegram: housing preparation

При выборе жилья в Цюрихе сравнивайте не только месячную аренду. Отдельно запишите дополнительные расходы, депозит, переезд и возможную оплату временного жилья одновременно с новой квартирой.

Если начинаете с временного адреса, заранее уточните в общине, какое подтверждение проживания она принимает для регистрации. Подготовили чек-лист переезда на английском:

## 4. Facebook: three registration routes

Arriving from abroad, moving between Swiss municipalities and changing address within the same city are different administrative tasks. Before booking an appointment, identify which one applies to you.

This registration guide explains what to check, which documents to prepare and what to ask before leaving the appointment:

## 6. Facebook: useful appointment questions

Before leaving your registration appointment, ask: Is my address registration complete? Will I receive confirmation? Is a separate permit or biometric appointment needed? What is the next deadline?

Keep the answers with your documents. Our Zurich checklist includes the official booking source and explains the difference between city registration and the cantonal Migration Office:
