# Sweezy SEO + GEO/AEO growth strategy

**Version:** 1.0

**Baseline date:** 3 August 2026

**Primary market:** newcomers and Ukrainians living in or moving to Switzerland

**Languages:** Ukrainian, English, German

**Primary outcomes:** qualified organic discovery, citations in AI answers, App Store visits, useful task completion
**Source of baseline:** six-month Google Search Console export supplied by site owner, Search type: Web

---

## 1. Executive summary

Sweezy should become the most useful Ukrainian-language knowledge system for moving to and living in Switzerland, supported by strong English and German topic hubs. Growth cannot come from metadata changes alone. It requires four connected systems:

1. **Search system:** technically indexable pages mapped to one clear search intent each.
2. **Answer system:** concise, dated, source-backed passages that search engines and AI systems can quote safely.
3. **Authority system:** real authors, reviewers, primary sources, external mentions and original datasets.
4. **Operating system:** ownership, review cycles, change monitoring, analytics and consolidation rules.

Current six-month baseline is 5,374 impressions. Reaching 100,000 impressions over the same six-month period requires 18.6× growth. Reaching 100,000 impressions every month requires approximately 65.7× growth from July's 1,523 impressions. These are different targets and must not be reported as one.

Recommended sequence:

- **0–30 days:** repair measurement, entity trust, citation structure and existing high-impression pages.
- **31–90 days:** publish and consolidate Ukrainian Status S, registration, work, insurance and canton clusters.
- **3–6 months:** launch original canton datasets, office directories and update trackers; earn relevant mentions.
- **6–12 months:** scale verified municipality and life-event pages based on Search Console and AI citation evidence.

No strategy can guarantee inclusion in 99% of AI answers. Control stays with search engines and model providers. Practical objective: become frequently cited source for narrowly defined Ukrainian-in-Switzerland questions, then expand topic share.

## 8. Content model

Every indexable guidance page should support these fields:

| Field | Required | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `title` | Yes | One clear user intent |
| `description` | Yes | Search snippet promise, 140–160 characters where practical |
| `summaryAnswer` | Yes | 40–80 word self-contained answer |
| `appliesTo` | Yes | Status, nationality, canton and situation |
| `effectiveDate` | Time-sensitive pages | Date rule became effective |
| `publishedAt` | Yes | Publication history |
| `updatedAt` | Yes after material change | Freshness signal |
| `reviewedAt` | Yes | Accuracy review date |
| `authorId` | Yes | Accountable writer |
| `reviewerId` | High-risk content | Qualified reviewer |
| `jurisdiction` | Yes | Federal, canton or municipality |
| `officialSources` | Yes | Primary supporting URLs |
| `changeLog` | Time-sensitive pages | What changed and why |
| `faq` | When real questions exist | Search and answer coverage |
| `relatedPages` | Yes | Cluster linking |
| `appAction` | When relevant | Useful product step |
| `status` | Yes | Draft, review, published, stale, archived |

### 8.1 Citation-ready page structure

Recommended visible order:

1. H1 matching user task.
2. “Коротка відповідь” answer block.
3. Applicability and last-verified line.
4. Decision table or checklist.
5. Step-by-step process.
6. Exceptions and canton differences.
7. Official contacts and sources.
8. FAQ based on real queries.
9. Related tasks.
10. Editorial and correction information.

### 8.2 Writing standard

- Put answer before background.
- Use exact dates, not “recently” or “soon”.
- Separate confirmed rules from proposals and expectations.
- Name responsible authority.
- State jurisdiction.
- Explain exceptions.
- Link source beside claim, not only at page bottom.
- Use tables for real comparisons, not decoration.
- Avoid unsupported superlatives.
- Avoid rewriting official pages without adding user-specific value.
- Never fabricate quotes, ratings, user numbers or government affiliation.

## 9. GEO/AEO citation strategy

### 9.1 Objective

Increase probability that ChatGPT Search, Claude web search, Perplexity, Gemini/Google AI features and Microsoft Copilot select Sweezy as supporting source.

### 9.2 What AI retrieval systems need

- crawlable, indexable URL;
- clear topic and entity;
- self-contained factual passages;
- primary-source support;
- visible dates and applicability;
- independent trust signals;
- internal and external links;
- stable canonical URL;
- fresh content when rules change.

### 9.3 Citation unit standard

Each important answer should fit in 40–120 words and make sense outside page context.

Example pattern:

> **Коротка відповідь.** Власники Status S можуть працювати у Швейцарії після виконання кантональної процедури повідомлення або дозволу, яка залежить від місця роботи. Роботодавець повинен перевірити вимоги відповідного кантону до початку роботи. Інформацію перевірено [date] за матеріалами [authority].

Required properties:

- subject named;
- action named;
- scope named;
- exception acknowledged;
- date present;
- source present.

### 9.4 Optional `llms.txt`

An `llms.txt` index can list the most authoritative Sweezy pages and explain site scope. Treat as discovery aid only. Google states that no special AI text file or special schema is required for AI features. Do not prioritize `llms.txt` above indexing, content quality or authority.

### 9.5 AI prompt benchmark

Create fixed benchmark of at least 100 prompts:

- 40 Ukrainian Status S prompts;
- 20 registration and permit prompts;
- 15 work prompts;
- 10 insurance prompts;
- 10 canton prompts;
- 5 branded prompts.

Test monthly across:

- ChatGPT Search
- Claude with web search
- Perplexity
- Gemini/Google AI mode where available
- Microsoft Copilot

Record:

- whether Sweezy appeared;
- cited URL;
- citation position;
- answer accuracy;
- competing domains;
- query language;
- date;
- model and mode.

Primary GEO KPI:

`Share of Answers = prompts where Sweezy is cited / eligible benchmark prompts × 100`

Secondary metrics:

- Citation Share by cluster.
- Unique cited pages.
- Citation persistence over three runs.
- AI referral sessions.
- App Store conversions from AI referrals.
- Incorrect-answer rate.

## 10. Technical SEO and AI crawler roadmap

### 10.1 P0 technical requirements

- Keep public content server-rendered and available without login.
- Explicitly document allowed AI search crawlers in robots configuration.
- Preserve `/api/` disallow.
- Test crawler access after every CDN/WAF change.
- Return HTTP 200 for canonical pages and permanent redirects for retired duplicates.
- Include every indexable canonical page in sitemap.
- Populate `lastModified` from real material updates.
- Exclude drafts, expired jobs, empty categories and thin generated pages.
- Maintain hreflang for EN/UK/DE equivalents.
- Keep visible text and structured data consistent.

### 10.2 IndexNow

Implement IndexNow for:

- new article publication;
- meaningful article updates;
- policy tracker changes;
- new or expired jobs;
- removed pages and redirects.

Purpose: faster Bing/Copilot discovery. Submission does not guarantee indexing.

### 10.3 Structured data

Recommended graph:

- `Organization` for Sweezy;
- `WebSite` for domain;
- `SoftwareApplication` for app;
- `Article` or `BlogPosting` for guidance;
- `Person` for real authors/reviewers;
- `BreadcrumbList`;
- `FAQPage` only for visible real FAQs;
- `JobPosting` only for live verified vacancies;
- `TouristAttraction` or `Place` for destination pages;
- `ItemList` for real collections.

Article schema should include:

- headline;
- description;
- image;
- datePublished;
- dateModified;
- author;
- reviewedBy where applicable;
- publisher;
- mainEntityOfPage;
- inLanguage;
- about and mentions when accurate;
- citations where supported by visible content.

### 10.4 Performance and rendering

- Keep critical answer text in initial HTML.
- Avoid hiding core facts behind tabs requiring client interaction.
- Maintain Core Web Vitals.
- Use responsive images with meaningful alt text.
- Prevent layout shift from video and large media.
- Keep mobile reading width and font sizes usable.
- Ensure every page works with JavaScript disabled for core reading.

### 10.5 Duplicate and canonical control

For every overlapping topic:

1. Choose primary URL.
2. Merge unique useful sections.
3. Redirect obsolete URL when intent is identical.
4. Keep separate only when audience or task is materially different.
5. Update internal links.
6. Remove redirected URL from sitemap.

Known review areas:

- Ukrainian job-search articles.
- Status S overview versus complete guide.
- Status S insurance articles.
- German insurance pages.
- Zurich canton guide versus moving-to-Zurich article.

## 14. Localization strategy

Translation alone is insufficient. Each locale must match different intent and terminology.

### Ukrainian

- Explain Swiss German/French terms in Ukrainian.
- Address Status S and Ukrainian-document scenarios.
- Use terms Ukrainians actually search.
- Provide original official term in parentheses.
- Prioritize reassurance through clarity, not emotional marketing.

### English

- Target expats, international employees and non-EU/EFTA newcomers.
- Use precise Swiss terms such as Anmeldung, Quellensteuer and Gemeinde where useful.
- Avoid competing duplicate pages for broad “moving to Switzerland” intent.

### German

- Use Swiss terminology and spelling conventions where applicable.
- Focus on local offices, employers and practical processes.
- Do not publish mechanically translated pages with incorrect umlauts or terminology.

Hreflang pages do not need identical content, but each language version must satisfy equivalent user intent.

## 16. Jobs and places SEO rules

### Jobs

- Publish only verified current vacancies.
- Use `JobPosting` schema matching visible fields.
- Include employer, location, employment type, language, salary when provided, deadline and application method.
- Remove or expire closed vacancies.
- Return useful expired state; use redirect only when replacement intent matches.
- Never index raw employer submissions.
- Avoid scraped listings without permission or added value.

### Places

- Keep practical route, best season, duration, transport and local context.
- Use distinct alt text describing real visible scene.
- Avoid copying tourism descriptions.
- Link places to nearby canton guidance and living context.
- Add first-party itinerary or newcomer perspective.

### 18.3 Content scorecard

Each page receives monthly or quarterly status:

| Signal | Green | Yellow | Red |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Impressions | Growing | Flat | Declining materially |
| CTR | Above expected position CTR | Near expected | Far below expected |
| Freshness | In review window | Due soon | Overdue |
| Sources | Current primary sources | One aging source | Unsupported material claim |
| AI citations | Repeated | Occasional | None after sufficient eligibility window |
| Conversion | Useful action occurs | Low volume | No measurable action |

### 18.4 Decision rules

- Do not rewrite titles after a few impressions.
- Test snippet changes after meaningful sample or four-week window.
- Expand pages ranking positions 4–20 before creating more broad pages.
- Consolidate pages with overlapping query sets.
- Refresh page when rule changes, not to manufacture freshness.
- Archive pages with no unique value and no strategic role.
- Keep pages with low traffic when they answer essential user or product-support tasks.

## 24. Route to 100,000 impressions

100,000 impressions across a six-month period is an 18.6× increase from this export. 100,000 impressions per month is a more ambitious approximately 65.7× increase from July.

Growth model:

1. **Existing demand:** improve page-one/page-two pages and CTR.
2. **Ukrainian authority:** own Status S, work, registration, insurance and canton questions.
3. **Geographic depth:** expand only municipality pages with verified unique information.
4. **Fresh inventory:** continuously maintain jobs, places, offices and policy changes.
5. **Original data:** publish trackers and directories competitors can cite.
6. **External trust:** earn mentions from relevant Swiss and Ukrainian organizations.
7. **AI visibility:** structure citable passages and measure Share of Answers.
8. **Operational discipline:** review GSC every 14 days and content freshness on schedule.

Near-term target: 5,000–10,000 monthly impressions. Mid-term target: 15,000–35,000. Long-term target: 50,000–100,000 monthly only after authority, inventory and maintenance capacity support that scale.

Desktop CTR should move from 0.36% toward 1.5–2.0% while rankings improve. CTR alone cannot create 100,000 impressions; topic coverage and authority must expand simultaneously.
