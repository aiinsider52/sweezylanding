## Route to 100,000 impressions

100,000 impressions is an 18.6× increase from this export, so it needs a content system, not one metadata pass.

1. Win current page-one/page-two terms: Zurich registration, Swiss tax return, Quellensteuer, Zug and St Gallen guides.
2. Publish Ukrainian clusters for work, Status S, permits, insurance, registration and canton offices.
3. Expand 26 canton hubs with municipality-office pages only where unique verified information exists.
4. Add jobs and places inventory continuously; expired or copied listings must not remain indexable.
5. Review GSC every 14 days: rewrite titles only after enough impressions, merge cannibalizing pages, add internal links from pages already receiving clicks.

Near-term target: 15,000–25,000 monthly impressions, then scale verified clusters toward 100,000. Click target depends on improving desktop CTR from 0.36% toward at least 1.5–2.0% while rankings rise.
