# Sweezy: path to 500 monthly visitors

## Definition and baseline

Business target: at least 500 measured unique website visitors per calendar month. Track organic visitors separately from other channels. Search Console clicks do not measure unique people; do not mark the visitor target complete from GSC alone. Analytics provider and production measurement remain to be confirmed.

Source: user-supplied Search Console Web export, 2026-03-17 through 2026-09-12. No page-filtered query export was supplied.

| Metric | Previous 28 days | Latest 28 days |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Dates | July 19-August 15 | August 16-September 12 |
| Clicks | 25 | 43 |
| Impressions | 2,601 | 5,190 |
| CTR | 0.96% | 0.83% |

September is incomplete. The 43 clicks in 28 days are roughly 46 clicks per 30 days, not 46 visitors. Reaching 500 monthly search clicks alone would require about 11 times that run rate. Illustrative arithmetic, not a forecast: 25,000 monthly impressions at 2% CTR produce 500 clicks; 50,000 at 1% produce the same. Neither guarantees 500 unique visitors.

## Release and measurement gates

Local verification: production build generated 336 pages; four report tests passed; browser checks passed for ten routes at 1440px and 390px, including canonical, single H1, indexability, console, overflow, redirect and structured-data assertions. Desktop/mobile screenshots were inspected for the tax article and Zurich checklist. This is not proof of ranking or production deployment.

1. Review and deploy the tested batch through the normal release process. Record the actual deployment date, not just the edit date.
2. Check live page status, canonical, rendered content, sitemap and redirect destinations after release. Use URL Inspection on the four Ukrainian owners and the two English priority pages; inspect Google's selected canonical and indexing reason.
3. Confirm existing analytics. Measure monthly deduplicated visitors with one provider, exclude internal/bot traffic where supported, and track App Store outbound clicks separately from installs. Do not install a placeholder tracking ID or extra tracker without checking existing consent and privacy settings.
4. Export queries filtered to the tax-return page and Zurich guide, plus country/device breakdowns. Aggregate page position is not the position for every target query.
