Start with one question: **is your tax deducted through payroll, or are you completing an ordinary tax return?** Keep your payslips and any tax-office correspondence together. Do not assume that one document replaces the other.

This page explains the system. For documents, filing steps and the distinction between the tax year and submission year, use the [Swiss tax return guide for 2026](/en/blog/swiss-tax-return-2026).

## What is Quellensteuer?

Quellensteuer means tax withheld at source. The Federal Tax Administration describes withholding for employees resident in Switzerland without a settlement permit and for certain employees resident abroad. Employers deduct the tax and transfer it to the responsible cantonal administration. Individual exceptions and cross-border rules require a separate check. The [federal withholding-tax page](https://www.estv.admin.ch/de/quellensteuer) also links to cantonal authorities.

A B permit alone is not a complete answer to your tax situation. Before asking payroll or the tax office for help, prepare your permit, residence dates, employment dates and household details. Ask which procedure applies, rather than assuming a salary deduction settles everything.

## Correction or ordinary assessment?

These are different requests. The following examples use [Zurich's official instructions](https://www.zh.ch/de/steuern-finanzen/steuern/quellensteuer/Quellensteuerpflichtige-Personen.html); verify the procedure with your own canton.

| Situation | Zurich procedure |
| --- | --- |
| Incorrect taxable salary or withholding tariff | Request a withholding correction. |
| Claiming additional deductible expenses | Request subsequent ordinary assessment (NOV), not a tariff correction. |
| Swiss-resident source-tax employee earning at least CHF 120,000 gross annually | Mandatory NOV; part-year salary is annualised for this threshold. |
| Other income or taxable assets | Check the canton's separate mandatory-assessment thresholds. |

Zurich sets **31 March of the following year** for correction and voluntary NOV requests. A valid voluntary NOV request cannot be withdrawn; resident applicants subsequently receive annual returns. Previously withheld tax is credited against the ordinary assessment. **A refund is not guaranteed.**

For a 2025 request, that March deadline was in 2026. Do not confuse it with an extension for submitting an ordinary return.

## How to compare tax rates

Avoid a single "Swiss expat tax rate" or a ranking of cheap cantons without matching circumstances. Use the [Federal Tax Administration calculator](https://www.estv.admin.ch/de/steuerrechner-steuern-berechnen), which provides individual calculations and cantonal tax data.

For a useful comparison, keep a record of your inputs:

1. Select the same tax year for both locations.
2. Use the actual municipality, not only a nearby city.
3. Check which income and household fields the tool requests.
4. Keep assumptions consistent between calculations.
5. Save the result and note any missing information.

Treat the output as planning information, not a replacement for an assessment. If the result differs from payroll withholding, ask which assumptions or procedures explain the difference.

For relocation tasks alongside your tax planning, see the [Zurich newcomer guide](/en/guides/zurich).

## Which deductions should you check?

Do not copy a deduction amount from another canton or an old article. The [Federal Tax Administration's tax brochures](https://www.estv.admin.ch/en/tax-brochures) compare income and deduction categories across federal and cantonal taxation; some detailed material is available only in German, French or Italian.

Build a document folder before deciding what to claim:

- Salary certificates and monthly payslips.
- Pension contribution certificates, where relevant.
- Insurance statements and any reimbursement records.
- Receipts for expenses you want the authority or adviser to review.
- Bank statements and records of additional income or assets.
- Previous assessments and correspondence about your tax status.

A receipt is evidence of an expense, not proof that the whole amount is deductible. Confirm eligibility, limits and the applicable tax year before entering a claim. Avoid choosing a pension product solely because an article promises tax savings.

## Questions to send your tax office

A short, specific enquiry is more useful than a general request to "reduce my tax":

- Which filing or correction procedure applies to my circumstances?
- Which tax year does the notice concern?
- What deadline applies to this particular request?
- Which supporting documents are needed?
- How should I report a move or income connected with another country?

Use official contact channels. Do not post salary documents, tax identifiers or bank statements in a public community group. Cross-border work, foreign property, self-employment or a mid-year move can need individual professional advice.
